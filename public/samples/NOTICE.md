# Bundled PDF samples

These files are included for local demonstrations and layout tests. You can replace either PDF at any time. To change its label or opening page, update `src/config/samples.ts`.

- **attention.pdf** — *Attention Is All You Need*, Ashish Vaswani et al., NeurIPS 2017, arXiv:1706.03762. The paper retains its authors’ and publisher’s rights. See its first page for the attribution and reuse terms for figures and tables. [Paper and publication record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)
- **sample-zh.pdf** — A two-page Chinese, two-column layout fixture titled 《基于自注意力机制的中文神经机器翻译方法》. It exercises Chinese glyphs, figure and table labels, equations, and references. The file supplies no separate author credit or license statement.

RefPin’s MIT license applies to its source code. Refer to each document’s own notices for reuse of its contents.
